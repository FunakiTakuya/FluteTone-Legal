## 1. Introduction

FluteTone (“the App”) is a flute tone-production practice app for beginners.  
This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Data the developer does not collect

The App is not designed to send personal data to the developer’s own servers for collection.

### 2.2 Data stored on device

- Practice history (timestamps, analysis results)
- Settings (reference pitch, and so on)
- Favorite target-tone information
- Local mouth-practice video when you use that feature

### 2.3 Payments

Handled via the App Store / StoreKit. The developer does not receive card details.

### 2.4 Permissions

| Permission | Use |
|------------|-----|
| Microphone | Pitch and waveform analysis |
| Camera | Mouth panel display and recording (Pro) |

### 2.5 Data we do not collect

The App does not collect location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

## 3. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell or provide personal data to third parties.  
Payments and distribution follow Apple’s services and policies.

## 4. Apple services

StoreKit and related services follow Apple’s policies.

## 5. Children’s use

Please use the App under a parent or guardian’s supervision when appropriate.

## 6. Changes

Updates will be posted on this page.
